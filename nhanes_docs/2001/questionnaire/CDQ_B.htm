National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey

2001-2002 Data Documentation, Codebook, and Frequencies

Cardiovascular Health (CDQ_B)

Data File: CDQ_B.xpt

First Published: May 2004
Last Revised: NA

Component Description

The Cardiovascular section (variable name prefix CDQ) provides personal interview data on symptoms that may be associated with angina pectoris or respiratory disease.

Eligible Sample

All participants 40 year of age and older are asked these questions.

Interview Setting and Mode of Administration

Home Interview; In-person.

Analytic Notes

Other than question CDQ010, these questions are not comparable to the CDQ questions for NHANES 1999-2000, which focused on symptoms related to congestive heart failure.

Data Access:

The CDQ data are publicly available at www.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/.
